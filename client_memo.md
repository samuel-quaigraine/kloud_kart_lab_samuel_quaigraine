## What I found

Kloud Kart can immediately stop website slowdowns, end weekly revenue arguments with Finance, and eliminate data privacy fines by activating our pre-approved duplicate server and setting up automated data rules. The website and database are not broken. Our issues occur because reports compete with live shoppers on a single computer, departments use conflicting spreadsheet formulas, and customer contact lists sit on staff laptops.

1. **Live Shoppers Compete with Reports:** Running Monday morning exports on our single server freezes customer checkout, causing roughly 800 abandoned orders and an estimated 280,000 Ghana Cedis in lost sales every month.
2. **Conflicting Spreadsheet Formulas:** Operations counts shipped parcels while Finance counts cleared bank deposits. This creates a weekly gap of roughly 150,000 Ghana Cedis and wastes over 400 executive hours per year in circular debates.
3. **Severe Regulatory Risk on Staff Laptops:** Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), when a customer requests deletion, we have ninety days to erase every single copy. With 300,000 customer records scattered across personal laptops and emails, Kloud Kart cannot pass a regulatory audit, creating 500,000 to 1,200,000 Ghana Cedis in legal exposure.

## What I recommend

1. **Turn on the second server this Monday:** Point all reporting queries and exports to the second server mirror immediately so shoppers experience zero website delays.
2. **Lock in one agreed revenue formula:** Replace personal spreadsheets with a single, locked database query so Operations and Finance report the exact same numbers.
3. **Strip personal customer details at the door:** Remove customer names, phone numbers, and addresses before data reaches reporting, ensuring full compliance with Ghana's Act 843.
4. **Hire a pipeline engineer first, then a report builder:** Hire a Data Engineer first to automate data flow and protect the store, followed by an Analytics Engineer to build clean dashboards.

---

## What I could not stand behind

- **Building an expensive enterprise lakehouse:** Lakehouses are built for giant corporations handling millions of video and audio files. For our forty gigabytes of retail data, it would waste vast amounts of capital on unnecessary software licenses.
- **Second-by-second live streaming dashboards:** Monitoring operations by the second changes zero daily business decisions and multiplies cloud costs. Daily morning updates satisfy our operational needs. The only number that must be real-time is physical warehouse shelf stock.
- **Hiring for artificial intelligence demand forecasting:** Predictive algorithms require years of clean, trusted history. Attempting machine learning on top of disputed revenue figures and uncertain inventory counts is a costly distraction until foundational data is reliable.
