[DETAILED BUSINESS CONTEXT & UNDERLYING MECHANISMS]:

  queries compete for the same physical processor cores, disk I/O channels, and
  memory buffers on Server 1.
* Architectural Solution (ADR-001):
  1. Activate Server 2: Configure the pre-approved duplicate server as an automated,

  2. Workload Isolation: Customer transactions write exclusively to Server 1. All
     analytical reads, Monday exports, and Finance audits execute on Server 2.
  3. Failure Mode & Fault Isolation: Even if an internal team runs an unindexed
     query scanning millions of rows on Server 2, Server 1 remains 100% responsive,
     keeping customer checkout latency below two seconds.

reports and Finance audits read from Server 2. The live store stays fast 24 hours

[DETAILED BUSINESS CONTEXT & UNDERLYING MECHANISMS]:
* Statutory Context: Under Section 33 of Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012
  (Act 843), customers have the legal right to request erasure of their personal
  records, with standard regulatory compliance expected within 21 calendar days.
* Regulatory Timeline: Kloud Kart faces an active complaint forwarded by the
  Data Protection Commission regarding a customer's deleted data, with a strict
  90-day internal implementation period to remove unmanaged files and prove
  system-wide compliance.
* Three-Step Privacy Engineering Architecture (ADR-004):
  - Step 01 (Pre-Load Privacy Scrubbing): Personal names, telephone numbers, and
    physical delivery addresses are permanently removed at the gate before data
    enters the reporting tables.
  - Step 02 (One-Way Anonymization): Customer emails are turned into irreversible
    anonymous reference codes (such as turning an email into `cust_98234`),
    preserving repeat purchase analysis and customer lifetime metrics.
  - Step 03 (Permanent Audit Log & Dual-Path Protocol): Every action is logged
    in `AUDIT_CUSTOMER_DELETION_LOG` (retained for 6 years, accessible only by
    Nana Adjei and the lead database engineer, with read-only inspection access
    for official auditors). This automatically re-applies deletions to restored
    backup files (removing), while permitting targeted recovery of purchase
    history via surrogate keys if an account was closed or contested in error
    (restoring).

[NANA ADJEI'S SPOKEN SCRIPT (SPEAKER NOTES)]:
"Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, customers have the legal right to request that
we delete their personal information. Today, because customer lists sit in
untracked spreadsheets on staff laptops, we cannot prove that records are truly
erased. Our architecture solves this in three clear steps. First, we strip personal
names, phone numbers, and addresses at the gate before data enters reporting
tables. Second, we keep anonymous purchase totals so sales analysis remains 100%
accurate. Third, we log every deletion in a permanent audit log retained for six
years, ensuring restored backup files never bring back erased records, while
still allowing us to recover past order history if an account was closed by
mistake."

[ANTICIPATED BOARD & INVESTOR Q&A]:

* Q2: If customer contact details are stripped, how does Marketing evaluate cohorts?
  Answer: "Marketing analyzes customer behavior through anonymous reference codes.

[DETAILED BUSINESS CONTEXT & UNDERLYING MECHANISMS]:
* The Streaming Complexity Myth: Real-time streaming data pipelines across an
  entire enterprise require complex message queues (Kafka), cluster coordination,
  and 24/7 maintenance that overwhelm small engineering teams.
* The Pragmatic Workload Split (ADR-003):
  1. 99% Daily Batch Reporting (02:00 AM Nightly on Server 2):
     * Financial reconciliation, marketing performance, regional analysis, and
       executive KPIs refresh automatically once every 24 hours at 02:00 AM.
     * Yesterday's confirmed numbers are stable, verified, and completely free of
       mid-day data drift.
  2. The 1% Operational Exception (Live Warehouse Stock on Server 1):
     * Physical warehouse stock balances are validated live on Server 1 during
       customer checkout, ensuring online shoppers never buy out-of-stock items.
  3. On-Demand Stock Drift Checks:
     * Warehouse supervisors can trigger on-demand stock drift comparisons on
       Server 2 during flash sales or payday trading weekends, identifying
       physical shrinkage without slowing down customer checkouts on Server 1.

[NANA ADJEI'S SPOKEN SCRIPT (SPEAKER NOTES)]:
"Investors frequently ask if we are building a real-time streaming pipeline. The
honest operational truth is that only our warehouse stock balance needs real-time
validation, so shoppers cannot buy out-of-stock items. For everything else
(financial totals, marketing metrics, and regional sales reports), refreshing once
a day at 02:00 AM is 100% sufficient. Executive decisions do not change minute by
minute; yesterday's verified numbers are cheaper, cleaner, and far more dependable."

[ANTICIPATED BOARD & INVESTOR Q&A]:
* Q1: Why not build a streaming pipeline now to future-proof our infrastructure?
  Answer: "Streaming infrastructure introduces high maintenance costs and software
  complexity that our two data hires would spend all their time debugging. Sizing
  for daily batch keeps our team focused on high-value business reporting."
* Q2: What happens if physical warehouse stock drifts during daytime operations?
  Answer: "Our nightly batch script compares physical warehouse scan logs against
  database balances, alerting warehouse staff at 08:00 AM to correct discrepancies
  before customer orders are impacted."
