RIGHT COLUMN: OUR PRACTICAL SOLUTION
1. Activate the Pre-Approved Second Server:
   - Create an automated, real-time mirror copy of our database on our pre-approved second server.

   - Store clean reporting tables inside a protected area on that same second server.
   - Requires zero new database servers or costly cloud subscriptions.

   - Replace private spreadsheets with locked, automated business definitions written in standard database queries.

[RECOMMENDED LAYOUT]:

[SLIDE TITLE]:
Protecting Customer Privacy and Meeting Our 90-Day Implementation Goal

[SLIDE SUBTITLE]:
Data Protection and Legal Defensibility under Ghana Act 843

[SLIDE BODY]:
SECTION 1: THE LEGAL REALITY (GHANA DATA PROTECTION ACT, 2012 / ACT 843)
* As I understand Act 843, customers have the statutory right to request the deletion of their personal details (with standard turnaround guidelines requiring action within 21 calendar days).
* Kloud Kart faces an active Data Protection Commission (DPC) complaint, with management setting a strict 90-day internal implementation period to prove full-system erasure and establish repeatable ongoing compliance.
* Core Architectural Law: Every copy you create is a place a deletion request must reach.

SECTION 2: OUR THREE-STEP PRIVACY PROTECTION APPROACH
* Step 1 (Sanitization at the Gate):
  As operational data moves to our reporting tables, direct identifiers (customer names, mobile phone numbers, and home street addresses) are permanently removed. Customer email addresses are converted into irreversible anonymous account codes (e.g. turning an email into cust_98234).
* Step 2 (Safe Business Calculations):
  Transaction facts (quantities, item prices, order timestamps, and cedi values) land in reporting tables untouched so revenue modeling works perfectly, but zero personal customer contact details leak into analytics tables.
* Step 3 (Permanent Audit Logging & Backup Protection):
  Every customer deletion is recorded in a permanent audit log (tracking anonymous ID, date, and reason without storing private data), allowing backup restores to re-apply deletions while permitting targeted recovery if an account was flagged in error.

SECTION 3: THE COMPLIANCE OUTCOME
* When a customer requests deletion, we only have to erase their record in one single place: the primary store database.
* Our reporting system is completely safe from privacy audits, and disaster recovery procedures automatically re-apply deletions so restored backups never resurrect erased data.

[SPEAKER NOTES FOR NANA ADJEI]:
"Under Ghanaian law, customers have the right to request deletion of their personal records. We face an active complaint from the Data Protection Commission, and our 90-day implementation plan ensures we wipe every unmanaged laptop copy, scrub personal details at the gate, and keep an immutable deletion audit log. We protect customer privacy, guarantee clean backup restores, and eliminate regulatory risk."

[SLIDE TITLE]:
Why We Update Nightly and Keep Stock Live

[SLIDE SUBTITLE]:
The Cadence: 99% Batch with One Real-Time Exception

[SLIDE BODY]:
CARD 1: 99% OF DATA UPDATES NIGHTLY IN BATCH
* The Strategic Test: "What business decision changes if this number is 10 seconds old instead of 24 hours old?"
* Reviewing weekly sales trends, evaluating marketing campaigns, and calculating monthly company revenue do not require second-by-second updates.
* Updating our reporting dashboards once every 24 hours (at 02:00 AM) gives us 100% accuracy with zero daytime server load and zero middle-of-the-night engineering alerts.

CARD 2: THE SINGLE REAL-TIME EXCEPTION: PHYSICAL INVENTORY STOCK
* Warehouse inventory availability cannot go stale; that is how e-commerce stores oversell.
* If a customer buys the last item because our stock count was delayed, we oversell, triggering refunds, customer complaints, and lost trust.
* The Policy: Real-time stock reservation stays live on the primary store database during customer checkout.
* The Safety Net: Automated nightly drift checks compare physical warehouse scans against database balances, alerting warehouse leads at 08:00 AM to correct mismatches before shoppers order.

[SPEAKER NOTES FOR NANA ADJEI]:
"A board member asked why our dashboards won't be streaming in real-time. If I am reviewing last week's marketing performance, a 10-second-old number changes nothing. Daily updates are completely sufficient and inexpensive. The only number that must be real-time is warehouse stock, so we never sell items we don't physically have on the shelf."

DELIBERATE DECISION: WHAT WE ARE CHOOSING NOT TO HIRE
* We explicitly advise against hiring a Data Scientist or Artificial Intelligence Engineer this year.
* Machine learning demand forecasting requires clean, trusted historical data. Predicting future demand on top of uncleaned inventory counts and disputed revenue numbers is an expensive mistake.
* Strategy: Establish our single source of truth in Year 1; evaluate artificial intelligence in Year 2.

Explicit Assumptions Stated Openly

What We Could Not Verify (Design Assumptions)

Because we formulated this strategy with available company records, we explicitly disclose the following design assumptions:

BOX 1: REVENUE FORMULA DEFINITIONS
* Assumption: We assume Finance reports net paid orders (cleared in bank accounts) and Operations reports gross placed orders.
* Action: Standardized SQL formulas will combine both metrics into one verified reconciliation ledger in Quarter 1.

BOX 2: CURRENT SERVER HARDWARE CAPACITY
* Assumption: We assume our single hosting server has standard modern commercial specifications (at least 8 processor cores and 32 gigabytes of RAM).
* Action: If smaller, activating the pre-approved second server is an emergency priority.

BOX 3: PAYMENT GATEWAY SETTLEMENT TIMING
* Assumption: We assume third-party card payment processors and mobile money providers settle customer payments on a standard 24-to-48-hour rolling window.
* Action: Nightly batch reconciliation schedules will align with these settlement windows.

BOX 4: NIGERIA PILOT DATA STORAGE
* Assumption: We assume Nigeria pilot orders currently write to our central Accra MySQL database (tagged by country code).
* Action: As I understand Act 843, core privacy duties apply wherever data is stored, and country-specific controls will align with Nigerian privacy rules as advised by legal counsel.

[SPEAKER NOTES FOR NANA ADJEI]:
"A defensible plan states its assumptions openly. We have assumed our server hardware meets standard specifications, that our revenue definitions follow standard e-commerce patterns, and that Nigeria pilot data is hosted centrally in Accra.
